FORV. exists for one reason.
The volume of scientific research being published has long surpassed what any individual researcher can reasonably follow. Important findings get missed. Cross-domain connections that could change treatment directions, accelerate drug discovery, or surface underexposed science go unmade simply because the relevant papers never land in the same hands at the same time.
FORV. runs a daily synthesis across major research databases, surfaces the most significant findings, and identifies the connections that tend to get lost when science is read within a single discipline.
It is free. It will remain free. There are no paywalls, no advertising, no commercial filter on what gets surfaced, and no agenda beyond the science itself.
The name comes from Forvitni, the Icelandic word for curiosity. That is the only compass this platform follows.
If you find it useful, share it with anyone who might benefit. That is the only thing we ask.
